THE DIFFERENTIATORS - what makes AskWRI different (and where you show it)
=====================================================================
D1  Verified, not just generated ....... every sentence is checked against its source, twice.
                                          Anything unsupported is removed before you see it.   (Step 1)
D2  Cited to the exact page ............ one click opens the WRI report at the page, highlighted. (Step 1)
D3  Written for whoever is asking ...... it works out the reader (researcher, policymaker,
                                          partner) and writes in the right form.            (Steps 1-2)
D4  Honest about limits ................ it says where reports disagree, what the evidence
                                          can't tell you, and declines rather than guess.   (Step 2)
D5  Shows how the research connects .... a map of which WRI report builds on which, and the
                                          key figures quoted word for word.                 (Step 3)
D6  Protects WRI's credibility ......... fact-checks a draft against WRI's own words before
                                          it goes out.                                      (Step 4)
D7  Built on WRI's own knowledge ....... 580 WRI publications, organised in WRI's topics,
                                          with WRI's own labels - even its charts and maps are
                                          searchable.                              (Tour, Step 5) 
D8  Ready for real use ................. runs on AWS with sign-in, and is measured against
                                          WRI's own targets on an admin scorecard.          (Step 6)
